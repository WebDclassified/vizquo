Concept & construction
The geometry is a system, not a drawing.

Two arms, one lens. The V arms are uniform 26-unit strokes at 27° from vertical, meeting at the apex. The lens ring (radius 22, 6-unit stroke) floats in the mouth with an even ~1.3-unit gap from the arms' inner edges — held, not attached.

Negative space is the product story. What you look through is the space between the arms — the lens is the aperture the instrument provides. At 16px the ring simplifies away; the V silhouette carries the identity.

Motion potential. The geometry is built for the signature animation: V → lens opens → inspection frame appears → hidden structure is revealed → returns to V. The mark is the first frame.
